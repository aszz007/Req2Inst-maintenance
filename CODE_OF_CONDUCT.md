# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and research-focused community.
Participation must be free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and
personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, code, and experimental claims rather than individuals.
- Be precise about uncertainty, reproducibility limits, and negative results.
- Respect data licenses, participant privacy, authorship, and peer-review
  confidentiality.
- Give constructive feedback and acknowledge the work of others.
- Keep public discussions free of private paper material, credentials, and
  non-redistributable datasets or outputs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted personal contact.
- Publishing private information or confidential review material.
- Misrepresenting experimental evidence, authorship, or licensing rights.
- Repeatedly disrupting technical discussion or ignoring maintainer boundaries.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers. Do
not open a public issue containing personal or confidential information.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, code review, and
other project spaces.
